Each chapter currently has four topics. That covers the core of each chapter and is not a claim of completeness.

## Inverse Trigonometry (Mathematics)
Covered: definition, domain/range/principal values; properties and identities; simplifying f⁻¹(f(x)); sums of inverse tangents and equations.
Likely missing:
- Graphs of the six inverse functions and graph transformations (needs diagrams).
- Formulas for sin⁻¹x ± sin⁻¹y and cos⁻¹x ± cos⁻¹y with their conditions; multiple-angle forms (3 sin⁻¹x, 3 cos⁻¹x).
- Simplification by substitution (x = tan θ, x = sin θ) and inverse functions with square roots.
- Inequalities with inverse trigonometric functions; domain and range of composite functions; counting solutions.
- Derivatives and integrals of inverse trigonometric functions (these belong with calculus but are tested together).
- Prerequisite lessons: trigonometric identities, one-one and onto functions, graph transformations.

## Binomial Theorem (Mathematics)
Covered: expansion and Pascal's triangle; general and middle term; properties and sums of coefficients; greatest term; remainders and divisibility.
Likely missing:
- Trinomial and multinomial expansions, coefficient of xᵏ in products such as (1+x)ᵐ(1+x²)ⁿ.
- Sums like ΣCᵣ/(r+1) and ΣCᵣxʳ by integration and differentiation.
- Roots-of-unity and complex-number methods for sums of coefficients at steps.
- Expansion for negative and fractional index (outside the official "positive integral index" statement; useful as extension).
- Binomial coefficients and probability or counting links.
- Prerequisite lessons: permutations and combinations, sigma notation, indices.

## Gravitation (Physics)
Covered: law of gravitation and field; g with height, depth and latitude; potential energy and escape velocity; satellites and Kepler's laws.
Likely missing:
- Gravitational field and potential of a ring, disc, rod and solid sphere (inside and outside) with graphs.
- Kepler's laws for elliptical orbits: areal velocity and the energy −GMm/2a, perihelion/aphelion speed ratios.
- Change of orbit, binding energy of satellites, geostationary and polar satellites in more depth.
- Two-body and binary-star problems; centre-of-mass treatment.
- Earth's shape and non-uniform density (conceptual).
- Prerequisite lessons: circular motion, vectors, work by a variable force (integration).

## Chemical Kinetics (Chemistry)
Covered: rate, rate law, order and molecularity; integrated rate equations and half-life; determining order; temperature, collision theory and the Arrhenius equation.
Likely missing:
- Complex reactions: parallel, consecutive and reversible first-order reactions; steady-state approximation.
- Collision theory detail: collision frequency, steric factor, threshold energy; energy-profile diagrams.
- Rate-versus-concentration and concentration-versus-time graph reading for each order.
- Catalysis (homogeneous, heterogeneous) and radioactive decay as first-order kinetics.
- Using titration, pressure and volume data (partly covered).
- Prerequisite lessons: stoichiometry, logarithms and exponentials, gas laws, basic integration.
